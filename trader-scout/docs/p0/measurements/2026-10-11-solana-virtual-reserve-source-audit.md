Those discriminators match the successful instructions in the quote-pool transactions. The same IDL defines `SellEvent`, including the final `creator_fee_unclaimed: u64`; the 449-byte event payloads in these transactions decode through that field with no unconsumed bytes.

The current official [PumpSwap trade V2 guide](https://github.com/pump-fun/pump-public-docs/blob/2293f9a66c654e9fe82dc5e8f4618538f24bb35f/docs/instructions/PUMP_SWAP_TRADE_V2.md) states that protocol and creator fees remain in the pool for v2 trades, while the buyback portion is paid during the trade. The [multi-hop guide](https://github.com/pump-fun/pump-public-docs/blob/2293f9a66c654e9fe82dc5e8f4618538f24bb35f/docs/instructions/MULTI_HOP_SWAP.md) says each hop emits its own event and the middle asset nets across hops. The [PumpSwap reserve guide](https://github.com/pump-fun/pump-public-docs/blob/2293f9a66c654e9fe82dc5e8f4618538f24bb35f/docs/PUMP_SWAP_README.md) defines effective quote reserves as the quote-vault amount plus signed `virtual_quote_reserves`, with raw base-vault amount on the base side. The [virtual-reserve fee note](https://github.com/pump-fun/pump-public-docs/blob/2293f9a66c654e9fe82dc5e8f4618538f24bb35f/docs/VIRTUAL_QUOTE_RESERVES_FEE_ADJUSTMENT.md) explains how retained fees are reflected in the signed reserve value.
