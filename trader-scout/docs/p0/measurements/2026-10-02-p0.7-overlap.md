# 2026-10-02 — P0.7 overlap experiment: GMGN leaderboard vs. our `buyer-intersect`

Question (TICKETS P0.7): do public leaderboards already contain the wallets our independent
on-chain discovery finds (→ be a strict verifier of leaderboards), or does independent discovery
find a different population (→ worth the Helius budget)?

Raw inputs, outputs and scripts: `docs/p0/measurements/p0.7-2026-10-02/` (reproducible offline:
`python3 overlap.py runB_ours_buyer_intersect_k1.jsonl`, `python3 wallet_stats_compare.py`).

## Design (fixed before looking at results)

- **Runner set**: GMGN `market trending --chain sol --interval 24h --platform Pump.fun
  --order-by volume --limit 30` captured 2026-10-02T13:38:51Z
  (`gmgn_trending_sol_pumpfun_24h.json`). Rule: top 8 by 24h volume among tokens opened ≤ 48h
  before capture (`runner_set.txt`). All 8 had already completed the bonding curve; all quote wSOL.
- **Leaderboard side (Tier 2)**: GMGN `token traders --order-by profit --limit 100` per token,
  13:4x UTC (`gmgn_top_traders/`). Cross-token set = wallets in ≥ K token lists.
- **Our side (Tier 1)**: `buyer-intersect --min-token-hits 1 --format jsonl` over the same 8
  tokens: confident bonding-curve buyers (ADR-003/ADR-009, qualification v4), Helius
  `getTransactionsForAddress` ascending from each mint's first transaction.
  - Run A: commit `40c690a`, 10 pages/token (1000 earliest txs), 256 buyers.
  - **Run B** (primary): commit `0ae2685`, `--max-pages-per-token 50` (5000 earliest txs/token),
    13:52:02–13:53:41Z, 400 page requests max, 1280 buyers, exit 3 (every token truncated by budget).
- **Profile cross-check (Tier 2, unverified)**: GMGN `portfolio stats --period 30d`, one wallet per
  call, for both K ≥ 2 sets (150 wallets, 13:54Z onward, `gmgn_wallet_stats_30d/`).

## Interpretation

1. **Leaderboards do not subsume independent discovery, and vice versa.** At the multi-runner
   level the two populations are disjoint (0 of 111 / 0 of 39). Per-token, our early-buyer sets
   catch part of a leaderboard (up to 60 of 100) but the leaderboard's cross-token "smart money"
   is not what early multi-runner buying surfaces.
2. **Our raw K ≥ 2 set is dominated by high-frequency early buyers.** A median of ~2,000 tokens
   traded in 30 days is bot/sniper/launch-farming behaviour, not a copyable discretionary trader.
   Per GMGN they are mostly profitable (91/111), but that profitability is likely speed-dependent
   and not transferable to a follower (AGENTS.md: copyability is not inferred from one win rate).
3. **So the P0.7 decision is "independent path stays, but not raw".** It finds a population the
   leaderboard does not, which justifies the Helius budget — but the product value depends on
   (a) an activity/frequency gate per cohort (P4.3: per-cohort quality gates) to separate
   discretionary early buyers from HFT/sniper bots, and (b) our own verified PnL (wallet-rank,
   P5), not GMGN's. Neither is built yet, so P0.7 is answered as a *direction*, not a final verdict.

## Limits (read before reusing these numbers)

- 8 tokens, one day, one trending snapshot; runner selection by GMGN volume ranking.
- Our scan covers only the first 5000 transactions per token and only the pump.fun bonding curve
  (post-migration PumpSwap/Raydium buys are not decoded); 79% of scanned txs failed (31,419 of 40,000) (launch
  contention), so 5000 txs can be minutes of a token's life. All buyer sets are lower bounds.
- Router-forwarded buys (P0.18) are never attributed; `positive_delta_without_instruction` is
  large for `GAwhcphC…` (1433), whose buyer set barely grew from run A to B (37 → 37) — it likely
  left the bonding curve early; not investigated.
- Every GMGN number is Tier 2 (third-party, unverified, never a ledger input). GMGN's
  `realized_profit` semantics, fee treatment and period boundaries are theirs.
- GMGN `portfolio stats` with several `--wallet` values returned a single object, so stats were
  fetched one wallet per call.
- Credit cost of the 400 Helius page requests was not measured (P0.6/P4.7).
