# PumpSwap legacy instruction-wire research (2026-10-14)

## Result

The available first-party, pre-observation package source does **not** explain the 6SFB/7rWY instruction variants. PumpSwap SDK `1.11.0` was published on 2025-11-17, before these December 2025 observations, and its bundled IDL is byte-for-byte identical to the pinned PumpSwap IDL dated 2025-11-15 (SHA-256 `d9d46f955118e7d71b2e7f64861a43e924e8693823f7660492d0ee7b8adc6c70`). That schema specifies `buy` and `buy_exact_quote_in` with two `u64` arguments plus `track_volume: OptionBool`, and 23 accounts; `sell` has two `u64` arguments and 21 accounts. `OptionBool` is a one-boolean struct. This confirms the canonical schema shape at that date, but does not identify the on-chain serialization used by each captured transaction.

The captured differences therefore remain unresolved. For 6SFB, 18 of 25 instructions differ: 14 buys are 24 bytes instead of the IDL's 25, four buys are 32 bytes, and one of those 24-byte buys also has 24 accounts rather than 23 (the additional key is the Rent sysvar). For 7rWY, 29 of 41 differ: 23 `buy_exact_quote_in` instructions are 24 bytes, four `buy` instructions are 32 bytes, and two `buy_exact_quote_in` instructions are 26 bytes. The first two `u64` arguments still match the independently decoded trade events, as recorded in the corrected replay. No source found in this bounded search assigns a meaning to the absent byte, extra bytes, or Rent account. They must not be interpreted as a missing false flag, padding, cashback, or a newer/older ABI without additional evidence.

## First-party evidence

- The [official npm registry metadata for `@pump-fun/pump-swap-sdk`](https://registry.npmjs.org/@pump-fun%2fpump-swap-sdk) records version `1.11.0` at `2025-11-17T11:15:02.377Z`, tarball integrity `sha512-vQliFcubxbI5x70QZYxuzqS5rKBEK2LtS3aVAzWzYPF6D4YkQz9XWi9VuKpJr6ghwJjwaxhTEMgNrdacAg5FcA==`, and git head `5c2646717d9e0c5b4a986620e615ef8edb006361`. The retrieved tarball's SHA-256 is `765a11c137cd94824dbd479a4a42bff77b75a50c6e9f45c61853ba410e5e1bc7`; its computed SRI matches the registry metadata. Its bundled `package/src/idl/pump_amm.json` SHA-256 is the Nov. 15 IDL hash above.
- In that IDL, `buy` args are `(base_amount_out: u64, max_quote_amount_in: u64, track_volume: OptionBool)`; `buy_exact_quote_in` args are `(spendable_quote_in: u64, min_base_amount_out: u64, track_volume: OptionBool)`; `sell` args are `(base_amount_in: u64, min_quote_amount_out: u64)`. `OptionBool` is declared as a struct containing one `bool`. The two buy account lists each contain 23 required named accounts; the sell list contains 21. The extra Rent sysvar key in one capture is not an optional named account in this schema.
- Registry metadata also identifies `1.12.0-devnet.1`, published `2025-12-08T19:07:25.309Z`, with git head `b993a05da20aead364b6dd72b72c67f499c4a0fe`. Its package IDL has the same instruction args and account counts. This is a devnet-labelled release published after the Dec. 4 observation; it cannot establish the deployed mainnet wire format for that transaction. Its tarball SRI also matches registry metadata.
- The registry metadata's repository field points to [`pump-fun/pump-swap-sdk`](https://github.com/pump-fun/pump-swap-sdk), but the direct official GitHub repository API returned 404 during this research. The official [`pump-fun` organization repository listing](https://api.github.com/orgs/pump-fun/repos?per_page=100&page=1) did not list a public PumpSwap SDK/source repository. This documents a source-access limitation for this search, not proof that no other historical source exists.

## Capture and limits

The corrected transaction-window audit is [the dated-IDL replay review](../../../.scout-data/diagnostics/solana-batch5-pumpamm-replay-dated-idl-v2/review.md); its frozen `instruction-audit.json` SHA-256 is `c7c64c9155037dc7cbbeacc079f2d0e7c7d198d4b72a7d97b97594a90bbe2b57`. It reports that decoded trade events, first two instruction arguments, account identities where matched, and vault deltas reconcile; the unresolved issue is instruction serialization, not event decoding. The replay covers only those captured transaction windows.

Private source captures and their hashes are in `.scout-data/diagnostics/pumpswap-legacy-wire-source-2026-10-14/manifest.json` (raw metadata and tarballs are under its `raw/` directory). The npm registry metadata/tarballs were unauthenticated public GETs; no project credentials or chain/API calls were used for this task. The 2025-11-15 pinned IDL and the 2025-11-17 SDK agree, but neither proves the deployed program's historical decoder or explains the 24/26/32-byte variants. No historical program binary/source-to-deployment match was established. Keep 6SFB and 7rWY pending rather than normalizing their unmatched bytes or extra account.
