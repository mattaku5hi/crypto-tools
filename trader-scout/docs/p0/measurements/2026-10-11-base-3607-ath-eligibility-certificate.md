# Base 3607 price-window and supply certificate

This check closes the known-empty-pool question for 3607’s saved Base V4 scope and verifies the May 5 GeckoTerminal high against a same-day carried pool state. It does **not** certify a canonical lifetime ATH: the same initialized pool has a receipt-verified May 4 execution price more than seven times the provider high, and its initial marginal state is higher still. No database value was written.

## Provider window and pool scope

The token has four PoolManager Initialize pools in the saved creation-to-anchor scan (Base block 52,456,236). All four GeckoTerminal responses identify 3607 as the base token and USDC as quote. Three contain daily rows; one contains none. The maximum high among nonempty histories is `$0.000388974286395251` on May 5 in pool `0x573a…fad65`.

| Pool | GeckoTerminal rows | Highest returned daily high (USD) | Date | Result |
|---|---:|---:|---|---|
| `0x4a54…5c96` | 4 | `0.0000679913547137323` | May 5 | Target-base/USDC metadata verified |
| `0x15a2…7455` | 2 | `0.000010054428911012` | May 3 | Target-base/USDC metadata verified |
| `0xcec5…dc8a` | 0 | — | — | Target-base/USDC metadata verified; bounded onchain price-state check below |
| `0x573a…ad65` | 4 | `0.000388974286395251` | May 5 | Target-base/USDC metadata verified; provider-window maximum |

The empty-history pool has one complete-range Swap event and zero hooks. Its Initialize marginal price is `0.000009999037550537377…` USDC per token; its only Swap’s emitted post-state is `3.69053317951293e-13`. The sole event has negative target-token `amount0` and positive USDC `amount1` (target sold for quote), consistent with the lower post-swap sqrt price; its fee is 900,000/1,000,000. With no hooks, V4’s [core swap implementation](https://github.com/Uniswap/v4-core/blob/main/src/libraries/Pool.sol) moves sqrt price monotonically in the swap direction; liquidity changes do not move `slot0.sqrtPriceX96`, and the fee does not reverse that direction. The initialized spot and the sole Swap’s post-state therefore bound the pool’s observed marginal-price path, though this marginal-state bound does not establish executable liquidity at the initial price. Kraken’s cached USDC/USD daily response covers May 3 through October 10 (161 rows, no missing dates or nonpositive highs), including the calendar day before May 4; the maximum daily high in this inclusive range is `1.0003`. Multiplying gives a conservative initialized-state upper bound of `$0.00001000203726180254`, about 38.89 times below the winning GeckoTerminal high. Thus this known empty series does not change the maximum within these four observed V4 pools. This does not establish pool discovery completeness across every Base DEX or factory.

The May 5 result is independently consistent with a carried state in the winning pool. Corrected StateView reads at blocks 45,577,802 and 45,577,803 show sqrt prices `1568644522115368171051` and `1021772282313318537305`; respectively, these match the May 4 last Swap and the first May 5 Swap. Its receipt hash equals the fetched May 5 block header hash, and the receipt contains the exact archived PoolManager log. There is no earlier Swap in that pool on May 5; the May 4 marginal state therefore persisted into May 5 until 01:35:53 UTC. Its 18-decimal target / 6-decimal USDC price, multiplied by May 5 Kraken USDC/USD low/high (`0.9997`/`1.0000`), is `$0.0003918858649691757–$0.0003920034660089784`; that is 0.7485–0.7788% above GeckoTerminal’s same-day high. The close numerical match is a **same-day state check**, not a May 5 trade at that price.

## Supply and price-definition blocker

Basescan marks the deployed `src/ClankerToken.sol` source verified. Its constructor mints the configured `maxSupply`; its other supply-changing entry points are `crosschainMint` and `crosschainBurn`, restricted in source to the Superchain Token Bridge, alongside ordinary burn support. The complete zero-address Transfer scan and pinned state show one creation mint, no burns, and total supply 100,000,000,000 at 18 decimals through Base block 52,456,236. No bridge mint/burn is observed, so supply stayed at 100 billion during the May 4–5 window.

The winning pool’s first Swap was May 4 at 07:53:23 UTC. Its Initialize record sets currency0 to 3607 and currency1 to USDC, with zero hooks. The first Swap has caller delta `amount0 = −306451339712177865084` raw target and `amount1 = +858060` raw USDC: target was sold for USDC. The exact gross execution average is `$0.00279998775925045214` USDC per token. May 4 Kraken USDC/USD bounds (`0.9970`/`0.9999`) convert this to `$0.00279158779597270079–$0.00279970776047452710`, or 7.18–7.20 times the GeckoTerminal high. This is the receipt-verified maximum execution ratio in the complete scan of all four known USDC V4 pools. The pool’s Initialize sqrt price separately corresponds to `0.5` USDC per token; liquidity at that exact initial state is not proven, so that mark is a scope warning rather than an executed-price claim.

A bounded StateView `getSlot0(bytes32)` call at block 45,545,927 returned the four-word all-zero slot0 tuple (128 ABI bytes). This is the block before the pool’s Initialize event, which occurred at block 45,545,928, log index 218; the first Swap is later in that same block at log index 265. The zero slot0 is expected before initialization and says nothing about liquidity or price between Initialize and Swap. It does not prove the `0.5` initial marginal price was active or tradable. No `getLiquidity` call was made, so active pre-Swap liquidity remains unknown; no `$50B` active-mark conclusion is justified. The retained request was mislabeled as a liquidity read: its selector `0xc815641c` is for `getSlot0(bytes32)`. The canonical selector for `getLiquidity(bytes32)` is `0xfa6793d5`; both signatures and return types are in the [official Uniswap v4 StateView interface](https://github.com/Uniswap/v4-periphery/blob/main/src/interfaces/IStateView.sol).

The [V4 event interface](https://github.com/Uniswap/v4-core/blob/main/src/interfaces/IPoolManager.sol) defines the Swap post-state sqrt price and fee fields. The [PoolManager](https://github.com/Uniswap/v4-core/blob/main/src/PoolManager.sol) emits the delta returned by `Pool.swap`; the [swap implementation](https://github.com/Uniswap/v4-core/blob/main/src/libraries/Pool.sol) constructs caller deltas with negative input and positive output. The observed Swap fee is 800,000, and [LPFeeLibrary](https://github.com/Uniswap/v4-core/blob/main/src/libraries/LPFeeLibrary.sol) defines 1,000,000 as the 100% maximum. The high fee helps explain why executed average and marginal spot differ, but it does not erase the observed execution ratio or validate the provider’s lower high.

A conditional provider-window calculation would be `0.000388974286395251 × 100,000,000,000 = $38,897,428.6395251`, floor `3,889,742,863` cents. If this observation is ever stored as a provider-window maximum, its exact intraday `ath_at` should remain null; GeckoTerminal supplies only a daily candle here. Under a price policy that treats observed swap execution or active pool states as ATH candidates, this certificate fails the maximum check and the conditional FDV must not be recorded as lifetime ATH. The existing competing execution value also means this requires an explicit price-kind decision before any broader use.

## Provenance and limits

The structured certificate, exact decimals, pool rows, state reads, and source hashes are in `.scout-data/diagnostics/base-3607-ath-eligibility-certificate-2026-10-11/certificate.json`. Corrected Base RPC responses are in `.scout-data/diagnostics/base-3607-may5-corrected-stateview-2026-10-11/`; the May 5 carried-state calculation is in `.scout-data/diagnostics/base-3607-may5-carryforward-audit-2026-10-11/analysis.json`. The initial four-request May 5 batch is retained in `.scout-data/diagnostics/base-3607-may5-stateview-2026-10-11/private-rpc-manifest.json`: its transaction receipt was valid and supports transaction/log identity, while its block header and two StateView calls used well-formed but incorrect heights and are excluded. The three corrected May 5 reads and the separate May 4 pre-initialization slot0 read are preserved in their diagnostic directories. This audit used eight Base RPC calls in total (one valid receipt plus three excluded wrong-height reads, three corrected reads, one pre-initialization slot0 read), with no provider, Codex, or database calls/writes. No liquidity read was performed. The price scope is the four observed V4 pools only, not every Base trading venue or a proven global lifetime ATH.
