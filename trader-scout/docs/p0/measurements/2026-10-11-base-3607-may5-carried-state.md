# Base 3607 May 5 carried-state check

As of 2026-10-11, a bounded read-only check confirms that the 3607 V4 pool’s May 4 closing marginal price persisted into May 5, before the first swap in that pool at 01:35:53 UTC. The carried pre-swap state, converted with May 5 USDC/USD bounds, is within 1% of GeckoTerminal’s May 5 daily high. This resolves the date mismatch for this one pool’s opening state; it does not establish a May 5 executed trade at that price or a market-wide/lifetime ATH.

## Evidence and method

The selected pool is `0x573a067f57088501085067df3336a523e5a498e028d9ec5c0e83ee05675fad65` at the Base Uniswap V4 PoolManager. The saved Initialize record identifies currency0 as 3607 (`0x3607f8…63b07`), currency1 as USDC (`0x833589…02913`), fee 800,000, tick spacing 16,000, and zero hooks. Its archived full-range Swap query contains five events from initialization through the pinned Base anchor; there are no other swaps in this pool before its 01:35:53 UTC event on May 5.

I issued three corrected read-only RPC requests: block header 45,577,803 and StateView `getSlot0(poolId)` at blocks 45,577,802 and 45,577,803. The block hash matches the first May 5 event’s transaction receipt. The pre-event StateView sqrt price (`1568644522115368171051`) exactly matches the pool’s last prior Swap on May 4; the post-event value (`1021772282313318537305`) matches the first May 5 Swap. The state therefore remained at the May 4 value until that May 5 transaction. An earlier four-request batch is retained separately: its transaction receipt was valid and supports transaction/log identity, while the block header and two StateView calls used syntactically valid but incorrect heights and are excluded from the continuity claim.

The Uniswap [`IPoolManager` event definition](https://github.com/Uniswap/v4-core/blob/main/src/interfaces/IPoolManager.sol) defines `sqrtPriceX96` as the post-swap Q64.96 square-root pool price and `fee` in hundredths of a bip. In the [PoolManager implementation](https://github.com/Uniswap/v4-core/blob/main/src/PoolManager.sol), the event emits the `BalanceDelta` returned by `Pool.swap`; the [pool swap logic](https://github.com/Uniswap/v4-core/blob/main/src/libraries/Pool.sol) constructs that caller delta with positive output and negative input. Here the May 5 log has negative target-token `amount0` and positive USDC `amount1`: the caller sold target for USDC. Its post-swap sqrt is lower, consistent with that direction. The event fee is 800,000 / 1,000,000 (80% under the V4 fee scale), also returned by StateView. This large fee is a material difference between marginal spot and the executed caller ratio; it is not used to adjust either price.

For a target token with 18 decimals and USDC with 6 decimals, currency1-per-currency0 spot is

`(sqrtPriceX96² / 2^192) × 10^(18 − 6)`.

The May 4 closing / May 5 opening pre-swap marginal spot is `$0.0003920034660089783590890456410041859152…` USDC per target. Kraken’s May 5 daily USDC/USD low/high were `0.9997` / `1.0000`, giving a May 5 USD sensitivity range of `$0.00039188586496917566558–$0.00039200346600897835909`. GeckoTerminal’s same-pool May 5 candle has open and high both equal to `$0.000388974286395251`; the converted chain-state range is 0.7485–0.7788% above that high, within the existing 1% comparison tolerance. The provider candle’s open/high equality is consistent with the prior state carrying into the UTC day; this check does not assume a specific candle-construction implementation.

The May 5 event’s post-swap marginal spot is `$0.000166321758179924495570200887…`; its caller execution ratio is `$0.000051067800478217027528…` USDC per target. These are distinct price kinds. A prior post-swap-only daily scan therefore missed the higher May 5 opening state because it is the **pre-state** of the first May 5 swap, not that day’s post-swap event. The May 4 state was present during part of May 5, not all day.

## Scope and provenance

This is a state continuity and same-day price cross-check for one initialized V4 pool. The full saved swap log range has five events, and the exact May 5 transaction receipt/header/log match is verified. It does not cover other Base DEXes, unsaved pools, or a token-wide maximum; it should not by itself promote a canonical ATH/FDV value. No Codex, price-provider, or database calls/writes were made for this check. The focused corrected continuity read used three Base RPC calls; across the full certificate follow-up, eight calls were made: one valid receipt plus three initial wrong-height reads, these three corrected reads, and one separate pre-initialization `getSlot0` probe. The three wrong-height responses are excluded from the evidence.
